How Shannon Entropy Measures True Resistance

Password security is not merely about using exclamation points; it is determined by the mathematical size of the search space that an attacker must exhaust.

Entropy Formula:
H = L × log₂(N)

Where L is character length and N is the available pool size. Each additional bit doubles the computational effort required for exhaustive search.

  • < 50 bits: Vulnerable to consumer GPU hash attacks.
  • 60 - 80 bits: Resilient against standard dictionary attacks.
  • > 90 bits: Computationally unfeasible against state-level clusters.

Modern Digital Attack Vectors

Understand how attackers compromise accounts and how CSPRNG stops them.

Credential Stuffing

Automated bots take username/password pairs breached from one service and test them against thousands of others. Reusing passwords guarantees multi-account compromise.

Defense: Unique password per platform

GPU Hashcat Rig Attacks

Modern 8x RTX 4090 arrays can compute over 100 billion NTLM hashes per second. Passwords under 10 characters are cracked in minutes regardless of symbols.

Defense: Length > 15 characters

Predictable Social Clues

Dictionary attacks utilize names, birth years, sports teams, or pet names scraped from LinkedIn and Instagram profiles using targeted regex masks.

Defense: Pure CSPRNG Randomness
